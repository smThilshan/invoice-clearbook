| 23 | Currency is cross-checked, never corrected: the model extracts a 3-letter code; a rule compares it with markers printed on the page and only warns. Hints only: `$` (usually USD) and a standalone D / D-with-stroke / Eth before an amount (OCR reading of the dirham sign). | 29 | Our own TRN finder (`verify/trn_finder.py`) reads the TRNs printed next to their labels (TRN, VAT/Tax Registration No, TIN, الرقم الضريبي; small scanner misspellings allowed; exactly 15 digits; excise/licence/phone numbers never) and decides whose by position (highest label on page 1 = supplier, even if unreadable) and customer labels.
